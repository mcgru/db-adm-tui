# Brief → PRD reconciliation

Sources: `brief.md` and `addendum.md` vs `prd.md`. The PRD builds on the brief and does not restate it, so delegation is expected; findings are limited to product-level omissions, contradictions, and unauthorized additions.

| Gap | Where in brief | Where in PRD (or missing) | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Effective-privilege inspection is one of three core views but has no functional requirement. FR-3 only lists users and roles; UJ-1 describes the missing screen. | § The Solution ("Inspect effective privileges in each engine's native terms"); § Success Criteria; § Scope | §2.1 FR-3: no consequence covers grants, memberships, or effective access. | **blocker** — SM-1 claims validation via FR-1–FR-4, but "what can this user do" is untestable and unbuildable from the PRD. |
| Bounded full-fetch contradicts the memory window. | § Scope: "bounded full-fetch mode" | NFR-5 caps each view at 10,000 rows or 64 MB and evicts old pages; FR-13 allows repeated 10,000-row batches and reports a "complete set" when none remain. | **blocker** — rows silently disappear, yet the status can claim completeness for data no longer shown. |
| Deployment envelope narrowed by invented OS/arch and engine-version floors. | § Executive Summary and § Success Criteria: stock hardened host; § Who This Serves: closed environments | NFR-11: Linux `x86_64`/`arm64` primary; NFR-13: PostgreSQL 14+, MySQL 8.0+, MariaDB 10.4+, MongoDB 6.0+. | **should-fix** — older engines are common on closed hosts; the brief set no floors. |
| Read-only blocklist expands beyond "write statements" and resolves a brief open question unilaterally. | § The Solution ("product guard"); § Open Questions: blocklist coverage undecided | FR-28 adds `VACUUM`, `ANALYZE`, `CALL`, `DO`, `COPY FROM`, `SELECT INTO`, `SET TRANSACTION READ WRITE`; FR-29 adds broad Mongo admin commands. | **should-fix** — may block legitimate pre-migration checks (UJ-3); needs product sign-off. |
